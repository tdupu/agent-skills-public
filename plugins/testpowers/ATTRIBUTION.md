# Attribution

Testpowers is original routing and contract work in `agent-skills`, informed by
the source register in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). It adapts the progressive
disclosure and adversarial skill-testing ideas of Superpowers and testing
conventions from the cited upstream projects. Those projects are not runtime
dependencies and their licenses remain with their authors.

The existing local ecosystem skills and package policies retain their own
canonical ownership. Testpowers routes to them when their predicates match.
