# Attribution

Docpowers is original routing and contract work in `agent-skills`, informed by
the source register in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). It adapts the progressive
disclosure and skill-testing ideas of Superpowers and the repository/package
documentation conventions of the cited upstream projects. Those projects are
not runtime dependencies and their licenses remain with their authors.

The existing local Mathcity skills retain their own canonical ownership and
policies. Docpowers routes to them when their predicates match; it does not
replace them.
