# Course initialization vocabulary

- **Course** — a subject with a stable identity that may have different catalog
  numbers over time.
- **Offering** — one course taught in one term, with its own confirmed policies.
- **Course format** — the teaching and assessment arrangement: meeting mode,
  classroom activities, workload, assessments, and the grade model.
- **Policy** — an instructor-approved rule for producing or maintaining materials
  within an identified scope.
- **Syllabus** — the student-facing statement of the offering's information and
  policies.
- **Reference** — a source used as evidence or background, not automatically an
  instruction or an adopted policy.
- **Historical rule** — a rule from a prior offering, awaiting an explicit
  decision about whether it applies to the current offering.
- **Course decision record** — the context, choice, rationale, and consequences
  of a decision about a course, with its applicable offering or offerings.
- **Course webpage** — the public page for one offering and the materials it
  makes available. Earlier pages for the same course are candidate baselines,
  including pages published under former catalog numbers.
- **Webpage baseline** — the existing same-course page whose site structure and
  presentation are copied when creating a missing offering page. Its historical
  facts and policies are not carried forward without current evidence.
