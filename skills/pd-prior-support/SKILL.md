# pd-prior-support

Panels read this section for two things: did the PI deliver, and is the PI
honest about what was delivered. It carries the merit case, so it must be
specific, and it must survive a reviewer checking every status.

## Required parts (check the current PAPPG)

Award number, title, amount, period; headings **Intellectual Merit** and
**Broader Impacts**; a **Publications** list (full citations in References
Cited); evidence of products and where they live; a **Relation to the
proposed work** heading.

## Intellectual Merit

- Open with the problem the award addressed, why it was hard, and why it was
  open — then the products, each saying what is new and what made it
  possible.
- One numbered item per product with its true status: published with venue;
  accepted / to appear; preprint. Do not name a journal for a paper under
  review or revision. Do not call a rejected paper "submitted".
- A result with a referee-identified gap is stated as what is proved plus
  the open step, not as the theorem with a footnote.
- Only work produced in the award period (check arXiv and acceptance dates);
  working notes that are not public are not products.
- No database sizes or project numbers that belong in the proposed research.

## Broader Impacts

Past-tense facts with numbers (subscribers, seminar years, visitors,
students and where they went, public repositories and licenses). Started
tools are "started", not "released", unless public.

## Rules

- Within the prior-support page cap; do not mention companion proposals.
- Every number and status has a `REVIEW.md` row from `pd-fact-check`.
