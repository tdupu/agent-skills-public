# gp-mentoring-plan

Required whenever the budget carries graduate students or postdocs
(Research.gov blocks submission without it; GRANT-FORMAT F16 predates
the student rule). One page at most, `proposal/tex/mentoring.tex`, 11pt,
1in margins.

## Contents (one short section each)

1. **Who.** The number of students or postdocs and the years, from the
   budget. Name the PI's past mentees with completion years. Take these
   from the PI's CV or COA Table 3; don't guess.
2. **Research training.**
   - How the person's piece of the project is chosen.
   - Meeting frequency.
   - Write-up expectations.
   - Code going into the public repositories with the PI's review.
3. **Communication and community.**
   - The seminar where they speak.
   - Visitors they meet.
   - Conferences or workshops, using the travel line.
4. **Use of AI tools**, if the proposal uses them: the person checks
   every claim; nothing produced by a tool is reported until a person
   has verified it.
5. **Career development.**
   - An annual written review.
   - Job-market advice and letters.
   - The university's professional-development resources.
6. **Assessment.** Papers, talks, milestones and placement, reported in
   the annual reports.

Register: direct and concrete (see `GRANT-STYLE.md`). Every claim is a
real practice of the PI.

## Finish

Build it and confirm that it is 1 page. Upload it with `gp-researchgov`
(Mentoring Plan), then re-run Check Errors.
