# pd-broader-impacts

Broader Impacts carries as much weight as Intellectual Merit and is where
proposals are weakest. Each item names something that already exists and
what the grant will produce, by when.

## Item shape

**Bold label.** What exists (with license, size, count, years, URL named in
words), then what the grant continues or produces, with a year.

## Items to consider (keep only those that are true)

- **Open infrastructure**: public repositories and their licenses; public
  databases and their review process; what the grant maintains or adds.
- **AI and software disclosure standards**: what a disclosure must contain;
  the checkers and record-keeping that enforce it; a release date for the
  template and tools.
- **Independent and amateur researchers**: a documented path to record,
  search the literature, review adversarially, disclose, and deposit AI-
  assisted work responsibly; release date; how it is taught.
- **Formal mathematics**: which open library receives formalizations and
  which roadmap the PI writes, by when.
- **Students**: what a student can own (a thesis-sized, public product); the
  PI's supervision record, stated at its true status.
- **Seminar, visitors, lectures**: years, counts, audience, planned content.

## Rules

- Never invent a deliverable the PI has not agreed to; flag any you propose.
- Private repositories are not "open source"; unlicensed code needs a
  license before it is called reusable.
- Numbers get `REVIEW.md` rows; dates come from the timeline.
