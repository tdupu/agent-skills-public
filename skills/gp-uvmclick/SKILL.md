# gp-uvmclick

Read `~/.claude/skills/using-grantpowers/references/browser.md` and
`references/uvm-facts.md` first. The PI signs in. Certify and
"Submit For Department Review" belong to `gp-spa-route`.

## SmartForm (Create Funding Proposal)

Fill every page from the proposal's facts; never invent:

1. **General Proposal Information.**
   - Fill the title, sponsor (NSF), and the activity type
     (RESEARCH – Basic).
   - Fill the start and end dates. Check the end date: the form defaults
     to 5 years.
2. **Personnel.** Add the PI. Add others only if they are named in the
   budget.
3. **Submission Information.**
   - S2S: No. Method: "SPA RA will submit via Sponsor's electronic
     submission system".
   - Who submits: the AOR, through Research.gov, with the temp ID.
   - Upload every final PDF under Submission Documents, and the SPA form,
     internal budget, COA and IBB sheet under Internal Supporting
     Documents, using the drag-drop technique.
4. **Budget Periods and Key Dates.**
   - Set the deadline.
   - Set the number of periods to the duration: use **Remove Period**
     until the end date is right, then re-read the page.
5. **IBB F&A Allocation.**
   - Does it include F&A? Yes.
   - Download the IBB tool (URL in uvm-facts) and fill it, giving 100% to
     the PI's department. Upload it through the "Upload" dialog (iframe).
   - Deviation: No.
6. **Review and Approvals.** PI eligibility, construction, F&A waiver:
   No, unless the PI says otherwise.
7. **Compliance Review.**
   - Human subjects, animals, radioactive materials, stem cells, NIH DMS:
     No.
   - Gain-of-function: "I Confirm" (the work is not dangerous
     gain-of-function research), unless the PI says otherwise.
8. **Additional Proposal Information.** Program income, cancer, SBIR,
   STTR, off-site research: No, unless the PI says otherwise.
9. **Completion Instructions.** Finish.

## UVMClick budget (workspace → Working Budgets → [Edit])

1. **General.**
   - Standard F&A: yes. Rate limitation: no.
   - Personnel inflation 4%, with **Inflate Period 1** checked when the
     NSF budget raises the salary before year 1.
   - General inflation 0.
2. **Personnel Cost Definition.** "Import Proposal Personnel" brings in
   the PI. Set "budget summer separately" to Yes. Add a "Staff Member To
   Be Determined" row for the Graduate Student (12 months), with the
   stipend base.
3. **Personnel Costs.** Click Edit, then for each period:
   - PI: summer effort 2 months, summer salary-request months 2, fringe
     rate (see uvm-facts).
   - Student: 3 months for a 25% appointment, fringe 13%.
   Click the grid's **Save**.
4. **General Cost Definition.** Add one line per category, from the
   cost-type list. Tuition goes under "Tuition & Fees", excluded from
   indirect.
5. **General Costs.** Click Edit, set the per-period amounts (e.g.
   publication in year 2 only, tuition rising), and click the grid's Save.
6. **Compare the totals** with the NSF budget. Report any difference
   above $15, and any default rate that differs from the NSF budget
   (e.g. the fringe rate).
