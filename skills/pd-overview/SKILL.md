# pd-overview

The Overview is the only page every panelist reads closely. It must say in
the first paragraph what the proposal will produce and why it matters, in
words a mathematician outside the subfield follows.

## Structure

1. **Headline paragraph.** One organizing idea, agreed with the PI (record
   it as an ADR). A strong shape: what the classical theory looks like and
   how it was found; what this proposal does in the new setting; what has
   already emerged (stated at its true strength); what is extended next and
   what is unknown.
2. **Solicitation paragraph.** Quote the solicitation's language verbatim
   (verify the quote against the current solicitation) and organize the
   deliverables under its terms — for example foundational definitions and
   theorems; databases and libraries; benchmarks; use of AI, computation and
   automation.
3. **Deliverable list.** One line per project: the object, theorem, database
   or tool it produces, with a section reference.

## Rules

- Lead with what the work produces, not with method or biography.
- Strength of every sentence matches `REVIEW.md`: "has emerged", "there is
  evidence", "agents report", or "we proved", never stronger.
- No "complete", "first", "novel", "transformative" unless verified and
  necessary; prefer the concrete object.
- Name who introduced what accurately; prior literature on the same objects
  must not be erased by "introduced".
- Write the Summary last, from the finished description: one page, headings
  Overview / Intellectual Merit / Broader Impacts, third person.
