# UVM facts for NSF proposals

Every fact here carries the date it was checked. Re-check anything older
than the current fiscal year before it goes in a budget or an email.

## Workspace facts

Identifiers (PI NSF ID, UEI, Research.gov org id, department code) and
current contacts (pre-award, SPA administrator, AOR, approvers, who has
left) live in the grants workspace's `UVM-FACTS.md`, next to
`GRANT-FORMAT.md`. Read it; update it when a contact changes.

## Rates (checked 2026-10-05; confirm with SPA each proposal)

## Salary

Use the PI's **salary letter** for the coming fiscal year, not the
public base-pay list (the public list omits raises). Ask the PI for the
letter if it is not in the folder. Summer month = 9-month salary / 9.
NSF caps senior personnel at 2 months per year across all NSF awards.

## People

Look up every address at `https://www.uvm.edu/people?search=<name>`
before any email. Never construct one from a naming pattern. The CEMS
pre-award coverage list is at
`https://www.uvm.edu/spa/cems-research-administration-services`.

## Deadlines

- SPA wants a submission-ready set **2 business days** before the
  sponsor date. Later than that, apologize, say plainly that a "no" is
  understood, and route anyway.
- NSF 26-520 (ANT) accepts proposals anytime and has **no target date** (checked 2026-10-05). Check the current solicitation's Due Dates section before stating any date to SPA.

## Forms and URLs

- SPA Pre-Award Request form: `NSF-three-year-template/SPA-2.0-Proposal-Preparation-Request-Form.pdf` (fill with PDF tools).
- IBB F&A Allocation Calculation Tool:
  `https://www.uvm.edu/d10-files/documents/2025-07/IBB_FA_Allocation-Calculation_Tool.xlsx`.
  Fill B10 with the PI's name, C10 with `CEMS - Mathematics & Statistics - 54040`
  and D10 with `PD/PI`, and put summer effort in the SUM row (2 months = 0.6667).
- UVMClick: `https://grants.connect.uvm.edu/`. Research.gov: `https://www.research.gov/proposalprep/`.
