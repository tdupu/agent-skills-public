# COA_template — Collaborators and Other Affiliations

NSF requires its own xlsx template (PAPPG II.D.2.h(iii)); do not retype it.
Download the current template from nsf.gov ("Collaborators and Other
Affiliations Information" template, PAPPG Chapter II) into the proposal's
`submission-docs/` as `COA.xlsx`. A grants workspace may keep a blank copy
beside its other templates (the reference workspace keeps
`templates/COA_template-2026.xlsx`); compare its header text with the
download before reusing it.

Fill with `gp-coa`. Tables:

| Table | Content | Window |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the person's affiliations | last 12 months |
| 2 | people precluded from reviewing (personal, family, business) | — |
| 3 | Ph.D. advisors and all Ph.D. advisees | all time |
| 4 | co-authors and project collaborators | last 48 months |
| 5 | editorial boards, editors interacted with | last 24 months |

Rows may be added; column sizes and font must not change. Single-copy
document, uploaded per senior person.
