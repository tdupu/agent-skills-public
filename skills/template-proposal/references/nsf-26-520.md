## Language to use in the Description

- §II: "Advances in formal mathematics, verification, and AI are creating
  new platforms for mathematical discovery. Proposers are encouraged, where
  appropriate, to take advantage of emerging tools that enable mathematical
  knowledge to be expressed in machine-readable form, facilitating its
  sharing, verification, and reuse. Proposals may integrate these platforms
  together with emerging AI capabilities to accelerate mathematical
  discovery. Proposals may also contribute foundational definitions,
  theorems, libraries, benchmarks, and other enabling tools to expand the
  range of mathematics these technologies may address when appropriate to
  exploring the mathematical frontier."
- §I (NSF priorities): proposals "that leverage emerging technologies,
  including AI, advanced computation, and automation, to make new modes,
  methods, or scales of science possible. The use of these tools should be
  scientifically justified rather than incidental." Also "the highest
  standards of scientific rigor, transparency, reproducibility, research
  integrity, and adherence to tenets of Gold Standard Science".
- §II "measures of success" a proposal should advance one or more of:
  publications or other scholarly outputs; improvements in student
  educational outcomes; undergraduates, graduates or postdoctoral fellows
  supported; students trained in a specific critical area; data sets
  established or expanded; new technologies or techniques established.
